# Format: email
One written email from the participant to a team member (or the team), submitted once. There is no reply in the transcript.

Default rubric dimensions:
- specificity: names the specific result, behaviour or change, with a fact, number or example, and says what is expected next. Strong has a concrete example and a clear expectation.
- tone: respectful, warm where it should be, firm where it must be; no sarcasm, threats or blame. Strong is respectful and suits the purpose (recognition or a concern).
- fairness: grounded in evidence, credits the right people, and does not single out or compare people unfairly. Strong ties the message to results or behaviour and treats everyone involved fairly.

An email that recognises one person while others did equal or better work is a fairness risk. A warning without a fact behind it is weak on specificity and fairness.
