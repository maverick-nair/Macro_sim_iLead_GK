# Format: presentation to a stakeholder
The participant briefs or presents to a senior stakeholder outside their team (an executive, the board, a customer's leadership, their own manager): where the team stands, the biggest risk, the plan and what they need. The stakeholder asks probing questions.

Default rubric dimensions:
- ownership: owns the results, good or bad, in the first person, and does not blame the team, the market or other departments. Strong says "I" and takes responsibility for the gap as well as the wins.
- honesty: gives the real picture with numbers, names the biggest risk and any shortfall plainly. Strong is specific and candid about what is behind and why.
- plan: says what will change, by when, and what decision or support is needed from this stakeholder. Strong has dated steps and a clear, specific ask.

A polished story without numbers is weak on honesty. Hiding a known problem from a senior stakeholder is weak on honesty and ownership. Blaming named team members is a red flag (blame).
