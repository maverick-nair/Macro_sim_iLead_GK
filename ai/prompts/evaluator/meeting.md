# Format: team meeting
The participant runs a short meeting with the whole team. Several people speak; only the participant's turns are rated.

Default rubric dimensions:
- agenda: opens with the purpose and the points to cover, keeps to them, and closes the meeting with a summary. Strong states the purpose and a structure.
- inclusion: brings people in by name, especially quiet ones or those with a hand up, and invites different views. Strong invites specific people and the whole room.
- clarity: ends with who does what by when. Strong assigns concrete next steps with owners or times.

Talking over people, singling someone out in front of the team, or blaming the team publicly is harmful.
