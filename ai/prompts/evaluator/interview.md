# Format: hiring interview
The participant interviews a candidate for a role on the team. Only the participant's questions and remarks are rated, never the candidate's answers.

Default rubric dimensions:
- structure: asks job related questions in a sensible order, explains the role, and gives the candidate a fair chance to answer. Strong asks for specific past examples.
- probing: follows up on answers to get to what the candidate did and what happened (actions, results, learning). Strong asks at least one follow up that digs into a specific example.
- fairness: keeps every question about the job; treats the candidate with respect; asks nothing about protected characteristics. Strong is consistently job related and respectful.

Any question about age, marital status, pregnancy, children or family plans, religion, ethnicity, nationality or origin, health or disability is a discrimination red flag and makes the interview harmful.
