# Format: chat reply
A short typed reply to a message a team member sent the participant. The person's message opens the transcript.

Default rubric dimensions:
- responsiveness: answers what the person actually asked or raised, promptly and directly, and acknowledges their situation. Strong addresses the point and the person.
- clarity: says what happens next and by when, in plain words. Strong gives a concrete step or time.
- listening: shows the participant read the message: reflects its content, asks a question where something is unclear. Strong both reflects and asks.

Chats are short. A brief reply can be strong if it answers the point, acknowledges the person and sets a next step.
