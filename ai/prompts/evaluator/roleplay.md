# Format: one to one conversation
A spoken or typed conversation between the participant and one team member (meet face to face, coaching, a feedback or role change talk, a reward conversation, letting someone go).

Default rubric dimensions:
- listening: asks open questions, acknowledges what the person says and feels, checks understanding, and lets the person's answers shape the conversation. Strong needs both a genuine question and an acknowledgement or summary of the answer.
- clarity: says plainly what the conversation is for and what happens next, with a concrete step, owner or time. Strong ends with a specific, agreed next step.
- involvement: invites the person's ideas and gives them a real say in the plan. Strong asks for their view and builds on it.

A good conversation fits what this person needs right now: more direction for someone new and unsure, more encouragement for someone capable but low, more room for someone ready to run with it. Rate the behaviour on each dimension; the style shown is reported separately.
