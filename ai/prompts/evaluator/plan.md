# Format: written plan and check in
The participant writes a goals plan for a team member, then discusses it briefly with them.

Default rubric dimensions:
- specific: each goal says exactly what will be done, by whom. Strong names concrete activities or outputs.
- measurable: goals carry numbers and dates so both can tell whether they were met. Strong has a number and a date for the main goals.
- involvement: the plan was shaped with the person: asks for their input, agrees the goals, and says what support the participant will give. Strong invites input and commits support.

A plan imposed with no room for the person's view is weak on involvement. Unrealistic or punitive targets set to make someone fail are harmful.
