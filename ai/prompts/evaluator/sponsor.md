# Format: sponsor briefing
The participant briefs their sponsor, a senior leader, on results against target, risks and the plan. The sponsor asks probing questions.

Default rubric dimensions:
- ownership: owns the results, good or bad, in the first person; does not blame the team, the market or others. Strong says "I" and takes responsibility.
- honesty: gives the real picture with numbers, names the biggest risk and any shortfall plainly. Strong is specific and candid about what is behind.
- plan: says what will change, by when, and what support is needed from the sponsor. Strong has dated steps and a clear ask.

Vague optimism without numbers is weak on honesty. Blaming named team members to the sponsor is a red flag (blame).
